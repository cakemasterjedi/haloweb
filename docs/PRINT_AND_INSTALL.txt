G30 TRUNK EMBLEM DISPLAY — PRINT & INSTALL NOTES  (88 mm housing, display loads from the back, one 16 mm hole)
=========================================================================================
Parts
  Halo_88_HOUSING.3mf               print BACK-DOWN (as oriented)
  Halo_88_BACK_PLATE.3mf            print FLAT SIDE DOWN, pegs up (as oriented)
  Halo_88_BACK_PLATE_SPREADER.3mf   instead of the plain plate if you fit the aluminium heat spreader (guide Appendix A)
  Generator: hardware/source/halo_housing.py (python3 halo_housing.py; needs numpy + scikit-image)

Print settings (ASA, enclosed printer)
  0.2 mm layers, 5 walls, 6 top / 5 bottom layers, 40% gyroid infill,
  flow +2-5 % (thick, slightly over-filled walls are what make the print watertight)
  Clean walls: outer wall ~40 mm/s, inner walls first, no part cooling for 3 layers then 10-20 %, dry filament.
  NO BRIM, no supports: the bottom edge is a 45 deg bevel and the board-pocket roof is a 45 deg slope.
  Textured PEI or glue stick, bed 100-110 C, enclosure closed.
  Back plate: 3 walls, 100% infill, cool on the bed so it stays flat
  Test-fit in PLA first: display in from the back until the glass touches the front lip, plate flush with the base.

Factory-look finish (optional)
  Wet-sand 400 -> 800 -> 1500, plastic adhesion promoter, filler primer, gloss black, 2K clear.
  Keep paint out of the lens pocket and off the base where the tape goes.

Hardware
  3" x 1/16" (76.2 x 1.5 mm) acrylic disc (lens, flush in a 1.5 mm pocket) + clear LOCA  (not B-7000: it crazes acrylic)
  0.64 mm 3M VHB 5925 (one continuous ring, 68-83.6 mm, cut with VHB_tape_template.svg at 100 %),
    adhesion promoter, isopropyl alcohol
  Black neutral-cure silicone, silica gel pack, conformal coating (MG Chemicals 422C or 419D)
  28 AWG silicone wire red + black, 30 AWG silicone wire x4 (all six through the centre hole)
  16 mm (5/8") step drill, 5/8" rubber grommet, touch-up paint
  12 V -> 5 V converter (YRDZXG 12/24 V -> 5 V 5 A 25 W), INA219, fuse tap kit with MINI and ATO taps + 3 A fuses in both sizes, ring terminal
  Optional: 12 V kill switch, 5 A+ (inline rocker on a pigtail)
  Optional heat spreader: 0.016" (0.4 mm) aluminium sheet (50 mm disc, 16 mm hole; Halo_Spreader_Disc_Template_1to1.pdf),
    soft insulating thermal pads 0.5-1.5 mm (3-6 W/mK), Kapton tape, 1.5 mm pad ring for the paint side

Wiring
  ALWAYS-ON fuse -> Mini or ATO fuse tap to match it (3 A) -> [kill switch] -> INA219 VIN+ ; VIN- -> converter IN+ ; converter IN- -> chassis ground
  Converter OUT+ -> board 12-pin pin 2 (5 V);  OUT- -> pin 1 (GND)
  INA219 VCC/GND/SDA/SCL -> board I2C port 3V3 / GND / GPIO15 / GPIO7
  All six display wires go through the 16 mm centre hole. Never feed 12 V to the board. Don't ground through the trunk skin.

Assembly (display unit)
  1 Make the cables; plug into the 12-pin header + I2C port; route the wires flat to the middle of the board.
  2 Conformal-coat the back of the board and both plugged connectors (mask USB-C, BOOT/RESET, keep off the glass).
  3 Optional: thermal pads on the hot parts + aluminium disc in the SPREADER plate (guide Appendix A).
  4 Display in FROM THE BACK, tab at 6 o'clock, until the glass touches the front lip. Ribbon loop into its pocket.
    Don't force it: find what catches and trim.
  5 Wires through the plate's centre hole; plate into the base (pegs out). It rests on the brass studs, flush with the base.
  6 Silicone around the plate edge and around the wires in the centre hole. 24 h cure.
  7 Confirm the phone control page works.
  8 Silicone the glass edge from the front. Lens into the 1.5 mm pocket with a thin LOCA layer, flush. Seal the lens edge.
  9 After 24 h: water test. Test tape ring on a tile, water to 2 cm below the rim for 30 min, then a garden
    hose for 2 min. No fog, silica gel dry. Fit fresh tape for the car.

Install
  1 Remove the factory roundel; leave the two grommets in the peg holes (the plate's pegs locate in them). Check they sit flush.
  2 Drill 16 mm at the exact centre between the peg holes (check behind the panel first). Deburr, paint the edge, fit the grommet.
  3 Clean the paint with IPA, adhesion promoter. One continuous VHB ring on the base (covers the housing base and plate edge).
  4 Wires through the grommet (seal with silicone), pegs into the factory holes, cable tab at 6 o'clock, press 30 s.
  5 Join the wires to the tray inside the lid. Let cure 24 h before washing.

Washing: hand, rain, touchless/brush car washes fine. Pressure washer: 25 or 40 degree tip, 30 cm (1 ft)+
  away, keep it moving, not on the rim edge, never a 0 degree tip. Sealed to IP67 practice, not lab-certified.
Heat: the board's ESP32-S3R8 is rated to 65 C air and the LCD to 70 C (80 C storage). The firmware's heat
  guard dims the screen from 70 C (chip) and turns it off above 85 C until it cools to 75 C. The optional
  aluminium spreader + thermal pads move the board's heat into the trunk lid.

Firmware: install from https://cakemasterjedi.github.io/haloweb (Chrome/Edge, USB-C).
  Phone: first power-up shows its Wi-Fi (EMBLEM-xxxx) and a temporary password on the screen;
  join it and choose your own password when the page asks.
  Power tab: voltage source Car battery (INA219), battery protection on, cutoff ~12.2 V.
  Auto-off: leave on NEVER. Once it turns off it stays off until the BOOT button on the board is pressed
  (sealed inside the housing) or a kill switch cuts and restores its power.
Finding an always-on fuse: lock the car, wait ~20 min, test which fuses are still live.

Key dimensions: 88 mm OD (over the 84 mm badge holder), 10.3 mm off the paint including the 0.64 mm VHB.
  Screen 2.65 mm above centre under a centred 3" lens. Pegs 4 mm x 6 mm, 56 mm apart. Wire hole 16 mm at the centre.
