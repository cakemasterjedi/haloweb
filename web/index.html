Battery protection

It wakes by itself every 10 minutes after a low-voltage shutdown and turns back on once the battery has recovered.

For the 12 V car battery, wire an INA219 module to the board's I2C connector (SDA 15, SCL 7, 3.3 V, GND) with VIN+ to the battery positive. It reads up to 26 V; no other analog pin is free on this board.

Level

  1. Before fitting: hold the emblem still with the picture upright and tap Step 1.
  2. Turn it clockwise about a quarter turn, hold it still for a second and tap Step 2.
  3. Once fitted, park on level ground and tap Level now. Rotation on the Emblem tab fine-tunes it.
